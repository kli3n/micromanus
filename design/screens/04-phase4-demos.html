The precursor bottleneck

Lithium sulfide is the constraint, not the electrolyte step itself. It runs roughly 40× lithium carbonate per tonne[2], and battery-grade material comes from very few plants[3].

  • Purity requirements above 99.9% rule out most industrial Li2S
  • Handling is moisture-sensitive end to end, which prices in dry-room capex[4]
Every announced sulfide line in Japan and Korea depends on the same three precursor suppliers.

Focus ring & contrast

D-70D-71D-69

The Phase 2/3 focus style was a 3px accent-soft box-shadow that measures about 1.1:1 against white — it was never actually visible. One :focus-visible outline rule replaces it everywhere. Press Tab in either card below to compare them.

before 3px accent-soft box-shadow ≈1.1:1 — fails

Tab through these. The ring is technically painted, but at 1.1:1 you cannot tell which control has focus — so keyboard operation is guesswork.

after 2px accent outline, 2px offset ≈4.8:1 — passes

outline rather than box-shadow, so the ring never enters layout or paint-area maths, and border-radius: inherit makes it track each control's own shape. Inputs keep their accent border as well.

The --text-3 contrast sweep

Anything the reviewer has to read moves from --text-3 (≈2.7:1, below AA) to --text-2 (≈5.3:1). --text-3 stays legal only for decorative or duplicated content. This is the single largest work item in the phase.
