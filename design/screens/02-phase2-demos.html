Summarise the high-risk system obligations under Article 6.
Research agent done · 7 iterations

High-risk systems under Article 6 fall into two buckets — those covered by existing product-safety law (Annex I) and the standalone use-cases in Annex III. The core obligations are:

  • a documented risk-management system across the lifecycle;
  • data governance with representative, bias-checked training data;
  • human oversight and post-market monitoring.

Enforcement of these provisions begins 2 August 2026 for most Annex III systems. [1]
